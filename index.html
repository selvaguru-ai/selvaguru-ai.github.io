About the app & support

iPhone & iPad · Free

Tree Decor AR

Life-size
in your room

Pick 4 to 8 ft, measured from the floor to the top of the topper. A 6 ft tree stands at 1.83 m on your real floor.

Decorate
piece by piece

Add, move and remove balls, bells, bows, toys, lights and garland. Undo any change. Choose a star, angel or bow on top.

A shopping list
for every ornament

When you finish, save a photo of the tree in your room and get counts and colors for everything you used.

Try before
you buy

No account

Open the app and start. There is nothing to sign up for.

Stays on your device

Designs are saved on your iPhone or iPad. No analytics, no ads, no tracking.

Real scale

The tree is placed on your real floor with ARKit, so the height you pick is the height you see.
