Tree
Decor AR

Place a life-size Christmas tree in your room, decorate it, and save a photo before you buy a single ornament.

Getting started

  1. Choose your tree height, measured from the floor to the top of the topper, and pick a style.
  2. Set how many balls, bells, bows, toys and lights you want.
  3. Allow the camera, then point your iPhone or iPad at the floor and move it slowly until the floor is found.
  4. Tap the floor to place the tree, then confirm it.
  5. Add, move or remove ornaments. Tap Done to save a photo and see your shopping list.

Requires iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 or later. Placing the tree in your room needs a device that supports ARKit world tracking.

Common questions

The app can’t find the floor.

Turn on more light and aim at a floor with some texture, such as wood grain or a rug. Plain, shiny or very dark floors are harder to detect. Move the device slowly from side to side.

I said no to the camera by mistake.

Open the Settings app, scroll to Tree Decor AR, and turn on Camera.

Save Photo doesn’t work.

The app needs permission to add photos. In Settings, open Tree Decor AR, tap Photos, and choose Add Photos Only.

My tree is gone after I reopened the app.

Your design is kept: height, style, decorations and where each ornament sits. The spot on your floor is not saved. Load your design from the first screen and tap the floor to place it again.

Is the tree really the size I picked?

Yes. A 6 ft tree is placed at 1.83 m from the floor to the top of the topper. The width shown is the tree’s skirt, not a measurement of your room.
