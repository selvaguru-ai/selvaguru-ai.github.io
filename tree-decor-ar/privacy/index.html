Tree Decor AR

Privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Tree Decor AR does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Camera

The camera is used on your device to find your floor and show the tree in your room. Camera images are not recorded or sent anywhere.

Photos

When you tap Save Photo, the app adds a picture of your decorated tree to your Photos library. It asks only for permission to add photos and cannot read your existing library.

Your designs

Your tree design is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Sharing

If you share your shopping list or photo, it goes only where you choose to send it, using the share sheet.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to bhuvanashere@gmail.com.
